About Book Explorer

Book Explorer is a small web app that helps you find books and save your favorites.

What you can do

  • Browse books from the Open Library
  • Search for a book by title
  • Save books to your favorites (they stay after you refresh)

Built with

  • HTML and Tailwind CSS
  • JavaScript (ES6 modules, fetch, localStorage)
  • Vite
